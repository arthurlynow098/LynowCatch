Match the composition of input 2 closely: ultra-wide 3:1 black near #090a08 background, a large rounded square mascot tile at left, huge heavy bold clean sans-serif logotype at right centered vertically, generous black margins. Replace the tiger with our existing octopus from input 1: preserve its recognizable ivory engraved illustration, curled tentacles, and indigo blue background. No white border or white corners around the blue rounded tile, no drop shadow. Exact single word text: LynowCatch. 'Lynow' in warm off-white and 'Catch' in vivid indigo/periwinkle blue. Fit all letters fully in frame. Keep scale and weight like reference, with tightly set type. No tagline, no subtitle, no watermark, no extra icons. This is the final production banner for the project's README. Desired canvas approximately 1800x600.
